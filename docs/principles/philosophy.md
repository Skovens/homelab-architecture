# Philosophy

Three ideas guide every decision in this homelab: a strong security model,
declarative infrastructure, and intentional simplicity.

## The Principles

- [Security Model](security-model.md) — rootless execution, per-service
  isolation, defense in depth
- [Orchestration Model](orchestration-model.md) — systemd as the orchestrator,
  declarative state, intentional simplicity

These pages capture the "why" behind each choice. The rest of the site documents
the "how" — specific configurations and implementations that build on these
principles. Where a deeper technical trade-off is at play, the relevant
[Decisions](../decisions/index.md) section holds the full architecture decision
record.
